# 更新日志 / Changelog

## 1.0.2

- 锉削窗口的钥匙预览支持按住左键左右拖拽，连续 360° 旋转查看；锉削更新后保留旋转角度。
- 选择内层或外层齿位时，在钥匙模型的对应位置显示红色立体边框；边框由 12 条棱组成，贴合剩余金属，随旋转和锉削更新。
- 修复旋转后的钥匙被窗口背景遮挡的问题，保留模型自身的前后遮挡，并适配界面缩放。
- 更新中英文锉削说明和验证文档。
- 继续支持 Vintage Story 1.22.x，使用 1.22.7 构建；通过 286 项自动断言。实际游戏画面仍需游戏内验收。

- Hold the left mouse button and drag horizontally to rotate the filing preview through 360°. Filing updates preserve the viewing angle.
- Selecting a tooth on either blade layer shows a red cuboid outline on the corresponding part of the key. Its twelve edges follow the remaining metal during rotation and filing.
- Fix rotated key geometry disappearing behind the dialog background while preserving model depth ordering across GUI scales.
- Update Chinese/English filing instructions and verification documentation.
- Continue targeting Vintage Story 1.22.x, built against 1.22.7 with 286 passing automated assertions. Live visual acceptance remains pending.
